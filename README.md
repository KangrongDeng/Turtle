# Turtle
## Turtle is an interactive app that brings environmental education to life. Players guide a baby sea turtle, clear debris, interact with marine animals, and help it reunite with its mother. 

   Turtle benefits children, teens, environmental educators, and anyone interested in conservation. Players see the ocean through a baby sea turtle’s eyes, clearing debris, rescuing trapped marine animals, and listening to their stories through interactive dialogue, ultimately reuniting with the mother. This participatory experience transforms environmental pollution into tangible life stories, allowing players to build emotional connections. Educators can use Turtle as an interactive teaching tool, guiding students in classrooms or activities to explore environmental issues firsthand.

    While designing Turtle, I aimed to make the interactive experience accessible to as many players as possible. The core story follows a baby sea turtle searching for its mother—a classic, easy-to-understand narrative that encourages player engagement. Visually, colors are optimized for contrast so that players with color vision deficiencies can clearly recognize task cues. Gameplay is simple and intuitive, requiring no complex gestures. All dialogue and prompts are displayed, allowing hearing-impaired players to experience the full story. Tasks are designed with clear clues to support players with different cognitive abilities. Turtle encourages players to build emotional connections through enjoyable interaction, learning to care for and protect the ocean.

     In Turtle, I integrated Foundation Models to provide on-device AI support, allowing NPC dialogue to move beyond fixed scripts and generate context-aware responses based on player input. This enhances interactivity and replayability. The user interface is primarily built with SwiftUI, while core scene rendering, interactions, and animations are handled by SpriteKit to achieve smoother 2D performance and precise node control. Finally, I used AVFAudio to add background music and sound effects, strengthening atmosphere and feedback, and making exploration, problem-solving, and interaction more immersive.
